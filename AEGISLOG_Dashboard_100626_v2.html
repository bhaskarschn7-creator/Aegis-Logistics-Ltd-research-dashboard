The business in four sentences

Aegis Logistics is India's largest independent owner-operator of LPG and liquid bulk-storage terminals at major ports (Mumbai, JNPT, Kandla, Pipavav, Haldia, Mangalore, Kochi), paired with an asset-light LPG sourcing-and-distribution arm (~90% of revenue, ~10% segment margin) [AR FY25 · 'Segments']. The crown-jewel terminal assets now sit inside Aegis Vopak Terminals (AVTL) — a JV with Royal Vopak, listed Jun-2025, in which Aegis holds 44.71% [Reported]. Terminalling is a take-or-pay annuity (liquid-segment margin 62.2% FY25); the investable idea is a ₹10,000 Cr gross capex build to FY27 (incl. India's first independent ammonia terminal) funded post-IPO from a net-cash parent balance sheet [Concall Q3FY26 p8 · CARE 2026 p1]. FY26 delivered record everything — revenue ₹8,333 Cr (+23%), PAT ₹1,107 Cr (+41%), throughput 5,152 kMT — but a rising minority share means owners' EPS (+35%) lags headline PAT [Provisional · IP Q4FY26 s12,s14].

Model & revenue streams

Gas Terminal Division (GTD) — ~90% of revenue at ~10% segment margin [AR FY25 · 'Segments']: LPG sourcing (Itochu JV), terminalling/throughput (take-or-pay), and distribution (packed/bulk/auto-LPG via 2,400+ distributor network). FY26 volumes: logistics 5,152 kMT (+14%), distribution 754 kMT (+45%), sourcing 607 kMT (+2%) — all records [IP Q4FY26 s11,s22]. Distribution margin stepped from ₹4,000 to ₹7,000/t, guided sustainable [Concall Q4FY26].

Liquid Terminal Division (LTD) — ~10% of revenue at 62.2% margin [FY25]: chemical/POL tank farms across 6 ports. Stable revenue, EBITDA −5% in FY26 — the soft spot [Concall Q4FY26].

Structure: terminal assets largely inside AVTL (44.71% held, listed, Royal Vopak partner, Itochu in HALPG); parent keeps sourcing/distribution + 44.71% look-through of terminals. NCI ₹2,729 Cr [IP s14].

Moat & market position

Largest independent port-terminal network for LPG/liquids in India; ~a fifth of India's LPG imports touch Aegis infrastructure [Estimated — mgmt commentary]. Moat sources: (1) scarce waterfront real estate + 25-40yr port concessions; (2) Vopak's global operating standard (77 terminals, 23 countries); (3) switching costs via pipeline/jetty connectivity — KGPL and Jamnagar-Loni tie-ins deepen it; (4) first-mover in independent ammonia terminalling (Pipavav 36,202 MT — India's first) [Concall Q3FY26 p5]. Demand tailwind: LPG demand 5–7% CAGR, imports +8–10% (mgmt) [Concall Q1FY25 p13].

Vulnerability: sourcing/distribution leg is margin-volatile and competes with PSU OMCs who are also customers — top GTD customer = 30% of gas revenue [FY25 'Segments'] 🟡.

Management, governance & capital allocation

Board/KMP: Raj Chandaria (CMD), Murad Moledina (CFO); promoter group (Chandaria family vehicles incl. Huron Holdings) 58.10%, zero pledge [AR FY25; no-encumbrance filing 07-Apr-26]. KMP remuneration ₹7 Cr (RKC) — modest vs PAT [AR FY25 RPT note].

Where the cash went (FY22→FY26): capex ₹~6,000 Cr cumulative (terminals; CWIP peaked ₹1,308 Cr FY25); dividends ~25–44% payout (FY26 ₹8.70/sh, ~34%); zero buybacks; M&A/restructuring via AVTL — HALPG 75% sold to AVTL (₹180 Cr gain FY25, balance FY26), J2/JNPA greenfield to be built then sold to AVTL [Events Oct-25→Feb-26]. Debt: raised through FY25 (D/E 0.99) then IPO proceeds flipped parent to net cash.

Pattern: disciplined on pledge/payout, aggressive on structure — value migrates between parent and JV via RPTs. Shareholder-ratified (postal ballot 01-Dec-25) but demands standing scrutiny 🟡.

Industry context & capacity footprint

India is structurally short LPG (imports ~60%+ of consumption) and storage-starved; PM Ujjwala keeps demand compounding. Static storage scarcity = pricing power for incumbents at berth-connected sites. [ValuePickr/industry commentary; mgmt]

Build-out: Mangalore 82,000 MT cryo LPG (commissioned Jun-25) + 194,382 cbm liquid; Pipavav 70,800 MT LPG + 116,620 cbm liquid + ammonia 36,202 MT (before Q1FY27); JNPA 'J2' greenfield 77,286 MT LPG + 318,100 cbm liquid + 35k MTPA bottling; Mumbai +61,000 KL (H1FY27); CRL-4 94,148 cbm (FY27) [Concall Q2/Q3FY26; IP Q4FY26 s9; Event 05-Nov-25]. New terminals start at 20–25% utilisation, ramp 7–10 yrs [Concall Q1FY25 p8].

Capex: ₹10,000 Cr gross / $1.2bn by Mar-27, +₹5,000 Cr by Mar-28, "$5bn road-map to 2030" [Concall Q3FY26 p8; Q4FY26]. Funding: AVTL Phase-II equity (SEBI-mandated within 3 yrs of IPO) + debt headroom.

Demand/supply: Middle-East disruption cut LPG sourcing ~50% in Apr → ~30% in May; normalisation expected by Q2 with alternative supply sources.

Capex/funding: $5bn through 2030 ambition; AVTL Phase-II equity pending.
